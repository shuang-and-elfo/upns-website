Where children learn and grow through play.

At UPNS, children spend their days exploring through joyful play: building with sand and water, creating art, listening to stories, singing, gardening, and discovering the outdoors. As they explore, they naturally develop curiosity, learn to solve problems, express their thoughts and feelings, and build confidence with friends. For 60 years, our cooperative community of parents and teachers has worked hand in hand to make school a warm, familiar place that feels like home.

Our Classrooms

Three classrooms for different stages of preschool.

Each room is shaped around the children’s age and stage, with experienced teachers, plenty of time to play, and parents helping alongside the classroom team.

Kitten Room

18 months – 3 year olds

Our youngest children explore water, sand, paint, blocks, and outdoor play. Teachers guide toddlers through smooth school transitions and partner with parents on potty training at your child’s pace.

Explore Kitten Room →

Kangaroo Room

3 – 4 year olds

Kangaroo children spend their days pretending, building, painting, climbing, talking, and learning how to play through ideas and disagreements with friends.

Explore Kangaroo Room →

Dolphin Room

4 – 5 year olds

Our oldest preschoolers take their ideas further through longer games, group projects, stories, outdoor challenges, and growing responsibility in the classroom.

Explore Dolphin Room →

School Memories

Traditions that bring families together.

Throughout the year, UPNS families come together for games, trips, camping, seasonal celebrations, and plenty of shared memories.

Family Fun Day (Spring)

Our biggest fundraising event of the year, where the playground comes alive with game booths, races, student exhibits, and raffle prizes.

Underwood Farm Trip (Spring)

A beloved spring field trip where children and families visit Underwood Family Farms to ride tractors, pick produce, and meet farm animals.

Parents Are Part of the School, Too

At UPNS, parents are part of the school. Families help in classrooms, attend meetings, care for the campus, and work with teachers to make the school run.
